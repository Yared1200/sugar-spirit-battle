Welcome to "Sugar Sweet Battle"!

Sugar Sweet Battle is a fun game where players choose from a roster, battle monsters, called "Sugarcreatures", and collect rewards.

What are the main features of the game?

  • Player vs. Player battles
  • Player vs. Monster battles
  • Collecting rewards
  • Leveling up your character

What are the mixes of the game?

  1. Mix of Prodigy Math and Pokemon
  2. Mix of Bollywood and Anime
